Hello! Welcome to My Little World of Terrariums

Hello! Do you like nature and terrariums? That is great, because I also do. I am Andrew — a young man who simply loves to craft and plant, and I've had this passion since I was just a child. Terrariums are a life in your hand.

Growing up, I spent countless hours outdoors, fascinated by the tiny ecosystems that formed naturally all around me — a cluster of moss on a damp stone, a fern uncurling in a shadowy corner of the garden, a spider spinning its web between two blades of grass. I was always drawn to the small, the intricate, and the living. At some point it dawned on me that you could take those small, intricate, living things and place them inside a glass vessel — and that the vessel itself would become its own world.

A terrarium, to me, is more than a decorative piece — it is a living, breathing world that you design, nurture, and watch evolve over time. From soft mosses and delicate ferns to tropical plants, exotic succulents, and everything in between, each terrarium tells its own story. No two are ever the same, and that is what makes every build so exciting.

I started this little corner of the internet to share my creations, techniques, and plant obsessions with fellow nature lovers. Whether you are a complete beginner who is curious about your very first closed terrarium, or a seasoned plant parent searching for your next project, you are in exactly the right place. My aim is to show you that building a terrarium does not have to be complicated or expensive — it just needs a little patience, a few good plants, and a genuine love of nature.

Over the coming weeks and months I will be posting build guides, plant care tips, container recommendations, and behind-the-scenes looks at my creative process. I would also love to hear from you — if you have questions, ideas, or simply want to share a photo of your own terrarium, please do not hesitate to get in touch.

Let's grow something beautiful together.

— Andrew
